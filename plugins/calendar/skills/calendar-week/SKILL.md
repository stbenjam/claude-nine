# Show the week

Use `icalBuddy` through the `calendar` skill to retrieve the next seven days.
Exclude holiday calendars unless requested, group events by day and calendar,
and identify overlapping or back-to-back meetings. Preserve event times,
locations, and calendars, and say when the schedule is empty or Calendar
access fails.
